Detailed Description

Level 1: a rule-based player that only looks one move ahead.

In order of priority it

  1. wins immediately if it can,
  2. blocks an immediate win of the opponent,
  3. avoids moves that hand the opponent an immediate win (playing right under one of his winning cells),
  4. otherwise plays the most central column.

Ties are broken at random. It is a surprisingly tough opponent for a human beginner and the building block of the "tactical" rollouts of MCTS.
