Detailed Description

Stochastic gradient descent with momentum. 

v <- momentum * v + gradient (+ weight_decay * w)
w <- w - learning_rate * v

The velocity v is a running average of the recent gradients: it smooths the noise of the mini-batches and speeds up movement along consistent directions. With nesterov the gradient is "looked ahead": w <- w - lr * (gradient + momentum * v).
