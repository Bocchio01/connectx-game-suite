Detailed Description

Level 7: classic alpha-beta search whose evaluation function is the value head of a neural network, instead of the hand-written "open lines" heuristic of AlphaBetaAgent.

This is the bridge between the two halves of the project: the search is the one of the classical agents (negamax, alpha-beta, exact detection of wins and forced moves) and the network only answers the one question search cannot answer by itself: "how good is this position for the player to move?". A depth of 1-3 is already much stronger than the policy head alone, and it is cheap enough to run on a microcontroller (depth 2 on a 6x7 board needs at most 49 network evaluations per move).
