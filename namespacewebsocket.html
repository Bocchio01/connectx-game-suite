Detailed Description

Just enough of WebSocket (RFC 6455) to be the server side of a browser connection: the opening handshake and the frames. There are no extensions and no sub-protocols, and the messages are text (JSON).

Everything here works on strings, never on sockets, so that it can be tested in isolation.
