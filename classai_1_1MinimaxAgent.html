Detailed Description

Level 2: plain depth-limited minimax with the heuristic evaluation of ai/evaluation.hpp. No pruning, no tricks: the number of nodes grows as roughly 7^depth, which is exactly why alpha-beta pruning exists.

The classic formulation is used: a maximizing and a minimizing branch, scores always measured from the point of view of the player who has to move at the root.
