◆ PolicyTarget

enum class ai::training::PolicyTarget
strong

How the label of a position is turned into a target distribution for the policy head.

Enumerator
BEST 

Uniform over the best moves (highest score: fastest win / slowest defeat).

OUTCOME 

Uniform over every move that keeps the game-theoretic result (all winning moves in a won position, all drawing moves in a drawn one). In lost positions: the best moves. Easier to learn than BEST (does not ask the network to pick the fastest win) and just as strong: a policy that never throws the result away never loses a won game.

SOFT 

softmax(score / temperature) over the legal moves: also tells how bad the bad moves are.

◆ playSelfPlayGame()

SelfPlayGame ai::training::playSelfPlayGame ( AlphaZeroSearch & search,
const SelfPlayOptions & options,
Rng & rng )

Plays one game against itself. Every position is searched with the network-guided tree search; the visit counts become the policy target and, when the game is over, the result becomes the value target of all the positions of the game (+1 for the positions where the eventual winner was to move, -1 for the loser, 0 for a draw). Only the search options (simulations, noise...) and the board of options are used.
