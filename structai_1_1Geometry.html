Detailed Description

Immutable description of a board shape plus every constant derived from it.

Cells are packed in a 64-bit word, one column after the other. Every column uses (rows + 1) bits: the extra, always empty, "sentinel" bit on top of each column stops vertical, horizontal and diagonal bit-shifts from wrapping around into the neighbouring column. 

.  .  .  .  .  .  .      <- sentinel row (never occupied)
5 12 19 26 33 40 47      bit index = col * (rows + 1) + row_from_bottom
4 11 18 25 32 39 46
3 10 17 24 31 38 45
2  9 16 23 30 37 44
1  8 15 22 29 36 43
0  7 14 21 28 35 42

Instances are cached per (rows, cols, connect) and live for the whole process, so positions simply keep a pointer to their geometry.

Whether the bitboard engine can handle a game configuration. Requirements: 2 players, rows >= 4, cols >= 4, 3 <= connect <= max(rows, cols) and cols * (rows + 1) <= 64 (the whole board must fit in one machine word: 6x7, 7x7, 7x8, 5x9, 4x12 ... are fine, 8x8 is not).
